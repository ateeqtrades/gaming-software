Questions

FAQ

How old do I need to be?

Junior karts are for ages 7 to 13 (minimum height 120 cm). Adult karts are for 14 and up (minimum height 150 cm).

Do I need driving experience?

No. Karts have one pedal to go and one to brake. Our marshals brief every driver before they go on track.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes and closed shoes. Long scarves, dupattas and loose items must be removed or tucked away for safety. We provide helmets and hair nets.

Can I walk in without booking?

Yes, if slots are free. Weekends and evenings fill up fast, so booking ahead is best.

How do I pay?

Cash, card, or mobile wallet at the counter. Group and corporate bookings need a deposit to confirm.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Free rescheduling up to 24 hours before your slot. Message us on WhatsApp.

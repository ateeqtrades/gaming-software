Three bars, stacked and widening. A stockpile seen from the side, layers of earth, steps down to the sea. It says what Bagh does without a single word: material, moved in volume, on a solid base.

Each bar is 8 units wider than the one above, centred on the same axis. The base is one unit taller, so the mark sits heavy. Gaps are always 3 units. Corners stay square.

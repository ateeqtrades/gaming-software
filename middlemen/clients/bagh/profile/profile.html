Bagh Group works through two companies. Bagh Mining & Minerals extracts, processes and supplies minerals in bulk. Bagh Import & Export manages international trade and logistics, from the source to the buyer's port.

To extract, process and export quality minerals to international standards, with ethical trading practices.

Our vision

To be a trusted mineral exporter from Pakistan, built on lasting partnerships.

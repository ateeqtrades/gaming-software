02 · The route

A second source.
From Karachi.

The Maldives imports all of its stone and sand. In 2023, 97% of its gravel and crushed stone by value came from two countries. When supply is disrupted, projects wait: in 2026 several were delayed by a boulder shortage. Bagh adds an independent route, direct from Karachi across the Arabian Sea.

03 · Quality and documents

Everything your engineer asks for, before you ask.

Imported sand, aggregate and cement need the Maldives government's Import Material Quality Approval, with test reports no more than two years old. We give every buyer the technical file for it with the first quote.

Ask for the technical file
  • 01
    Lab report per lotGrading, silt and clay, chlorides, sulphates, density and absorption
  • 02
    Third party inspectionIndependent surveyor at loading, with sealed reference samples kept by both sides
  • 03
    Armour rock grading certificateBlock weight distribution and density to EN 13383
  • 04
    Export papersCertificate of origin, export clearance, mining lease and royalty documents
  • 05
    Commercial terms in writingPrice validity, LC or advance terms, minimum order and loading calendar
